# Example Financial Services Cybersecurity Standard 2026

Section 1.1: The organisation shall establish an accountable information security governance structure approved by management.

Section 2.1: The organisation shall restrict privileged system access to authorised personnel and review privileged accounts quarterly.

Section 3.4: The organisation must maintain an incident response process and notify the regulator within 72 hours when a reportable breach occurs.

Section 4.2: The organisation shall assess cybersecurity risk annually and document approved treatment actions.

Section 5.1: Personnel should receive security awareness training annually.

Section 6.3: The organisation shall assess critical third-party service providers before onboarding and periodically thereafter.
